Marks sit only in the paper margin, so nothing shows on the finished cards.

Print, cut, fold

  1. Print on letter paper at 100% / “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”
  2. Cut between the solid marks in the margin, using a ruler or paper trimmer
  3. Fold between the dashed marks, with the printing on the outside. Scoring along a ruler first makes it crisp
  4. Lay the blank flap over the bottom panel and glue or staple it
