Tips & shortcuts
  • Drag guests onto seats; drop them back here to unseat
  • Drop a seated guest on another seat to swap
  • Double-click a seated guest to unseat
  • Right-click a guest to rename or regroup
  • Drag a guest onto a group to tag them
  • Drag a tabletop to move it; double-click to rename
  • Drag the floor to pan, scroll to zoom
  • Search also finds seated guests; click one to fly to their seat
  • Hover a group to light up its seats
